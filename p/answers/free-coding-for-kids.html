Is there genuinely free coding for kids?

There is a lot genuinely available for free, and also a lot of 'free' that means a trial, a demo, or an ad-supported product aimed at your child. It is worth knowing which you are looking at.

The four kinds of free

A permanently free tier with real content. A time-limited trial. A free product paid for by advertising. And free-to-start where the useful part is locked. All four get described the same way in marketing, so check which one you are being offered before your child gets attached.

Ads are the one to watch

For a children's product, advertising is not just an annoyance - it means your child is the thing being monetised, and it usually implies tracking. If a kids' platform is free and ad-supported, read the privacy policy specifically for what is shared with third parties.

What to check before signing up

Does it require a parent to approve the account? Does it sell or share data? Are there public galleries or messaging between users? Can you delete your child's data on request? Those four answers tell you more than any feature list.

Free doesn't have to mean worse

Plenty of excellent material is free, particularly for beginners. The paid tiers usually buy depth, progress tracking and support rather than fundamentally better teaching at the start.
