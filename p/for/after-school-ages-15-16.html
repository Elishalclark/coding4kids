Coding for after-school programs (ages 15-16)

After-school is chaotic by nature: kids arrive late, attendance rotates, and staff change week to week. Self-paced tracks absorb all of that.

What ages 15-16 looks like

Depth and portfolio work. Older beginners move fast and should skip the scaffolding entirely - starting at typed code from day one. Most children this age are working through AI Island, Data Structures Dojo and Master's Summit.

Nobody falls behind by missing a week

Each child resumes exactly where they left off. There is no lesson plan to keep the group synchronised with, so inconsistent attendance stops being a problem.

New staff can run it on day one

There is nothing to prepare and no material to learn ahead of the session. A new staff member can supervise competently the first time.

Short, finishable lessons

Every lesson has a visible end point, so a child who only has twenty minutes still finishes something rather than stopping mid-task.

Safe with light supervision

No ads, no public gallery, no messaging between children.
