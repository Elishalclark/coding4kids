Coding for classrooms (ages 15-16)

Built for a teacher who has twenty-five students at fifteen different levels and one period to work with.

What ages 15-16 looks like

Depth and portfolio work. Older beginners move fast and should skip the scaffolding entirely - starting at typed code from day one. Most children this age are working through AI Island, Data Structures Dojo and Master's Summit.

Roster upload and class codes

Add students in bulk from a CSV, or hand out a class code and let them join themselves.

Assign lessons with due dates

Set what the class should cover and track who has finished, without forcing everyone through the same lesson on the same day.

School hours

Restrict when students can log in, so the platform is available during your period and not at midnight.

Progress reports you can download

Per-student completion and quiz results, exportable for your own records or for parents evening.
