Coding for homeschoolers (ages 9-11)

You don't need to know how to code to teach it. KidVibers is fully self-paced, checks every answer itself, and keeps the records you need for reporting.

What ages 9-11 looks like

The switch to typed code. Sessions stretch to 20-30 minutes and most children can work independently, coming to you only when stuck. Most children this age are working through Cosmic Code Station, Algorithm Castle and Game Arcade.

You are not the teacher here

Each lesson explains its own concept, the child writes real code, and the platform checks the work. A built-in helper gives hints when they're stuck, so progress doesn't stall waiting for you.

Records for your portfolio

Lessons completed, quiz results and printable certificates - dated and per child. If your state asks for evidence of learning, it's already assembled.

One plan, several children

A Family plan covers multiple kids, each on their own track at their own level, so siblings years apart can work at the same table.

Works when the internet doesn't

Lessons cache to the device, so it keeps working on a trip, at a cabin, or on rural internet that comes and goes.

KidVibers covers ages 6-16 across 293 lessons and 25 worlds. There is a free tier and a free trial.
