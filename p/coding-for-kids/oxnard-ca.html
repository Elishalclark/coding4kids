Coding for kids in Oxnard, CA

KidVibers is an online coding platform for children aged 6-16. Families in Oxnard, CA can use it from home, at any time - there is no class to travel to and no schedule to fit around.

Learn from home, on your own schedule

Everything runs in the browser, so children in Oxnard, CA work through the same 293 lessons at their own pace rather than at a fixed class time. That suits families juggling school, homeschool co-ops and after-school activities.

No coding knowledge needed at home
