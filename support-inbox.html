📥 Support Inbox

Messages captured from support@kidvibers.com.

⚠️ This inbox only fills up once inbound email is wired up. This page and its API are ready, but actually routing mail sent to support@kidvibers.com here requires a one-time setup step outside this codebase:

1. Set an INBOUND_EMAIL_SECRET environment variable on the server (any long random string).
2. Configure an inbound-email provider to POST each message to POST /api/inbound/support-email as JSON: {"secret":"...","from":"...","subject":"...","text":"..."}. Options: Cloudflare Email Routing (forward support@ to a small Worker that POSTs here — a natural fit since KidVibers already uses Cloudflare), or an inbound-parse webhook from your email/DNS provider (e.g. Resend, Mailgun).

Until that's connected, support@ email still arrives in your normal mailbox exactly as it does today — nothing about your current email is changed.
