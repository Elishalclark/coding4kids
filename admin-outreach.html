    ✉️ Outreach templates

    Pick the type, edit if you like, then press ✉️ Send on a row below — this panel is the message that gets sent. {{ORG}} and {{NAME}} are filled in automatically for each organisation, so you write it once and it comes out personalised.

    The ✉️ Send button only appears on rows that have a contact email saved. The starter list ships without addresses on purpose — guessing one bounces, and bounces damage the sender reputation your parental-consent emails depend on. Open the org's website, find a named person, then press ✉️ Add email first on that row.

    🔎 Where to find organisations

    These directories list real organisations with real contact pages. Work through one, add the ones that fit, and email them from here. Aim for 10–20 a week rather than a big push.

    • HSLDA — State Homeschool Organizations
      The most complete list of state-level homeschool associations. Start here.
    • Freedom Homeschooling — State Organizations
      Second list, overlaps with HSLDA but catches some it misses.
    • Homeschool support groups & co-ops, state by state
      Local co-ops. Smaller, but they answer faster than the big associations.
    • ALSC — Association for Library Service to Children
      For libraries, the person you want is the youth services or children's librarian, not the director.
    • IMLS Public Libraries Survey
      Every public library system in the US as open data, with location and size. Good for finding systems near you.
    Two rules worth keeping.
    Use a real named contact wherever you can find one — info@ addresses get ignored, a named youth services librarian does not.
    Never guess an address. A bounce costs you sender reputation, and reputation is what gets your parental-consent emails delivered.
